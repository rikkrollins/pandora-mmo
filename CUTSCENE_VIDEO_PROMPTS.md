# Cutscene video-generation prompts

Running list, kept over time (per Coffee, 2026-09-26): candidate moments
from the Chapters 1-8 narrative expansion project worth turning into a
real short video via an external AI video generator. Each entry is a
**visual** prompt (subject/action/setting/mood/camera/lighting) meant
to be pasted into a text-to-video tool directly — not the in-game
narration text itself, which lives in `ai/dm_agent.py` and is quoted
here only for context on what the video should accompany.

No trademarked names/IP references in any prompt below, per this
repo's own standing rule (`CLAUDE.md`) — every prompt describes only
this game's own original content.

**Workflow**: Coffee runs a prompt below through his own AI video tool,
then hands the resulting file back for integration into the matching
scene. Entries are marked `[ ]` (not yet generated) / `[x]` (video
received and integrated) — update the checkbox and note the real
in-game hookup point (function name + file) once a video is wired in.

## Chapter 1 — Discovery

- [ ] **Whispering Wood arrival** (`crossroads_tavern_opening_scenes`,
  `ai/dm_agent.py`) — A narrow dirt path through dense, silent forest
  at dusk. No birds, no wind sound. Camera pushes slowly forward down
  the path toward an old wooden bridge that doesn't move or creak.
  Something unseen watches from the treeline, unhurried. Muted greens
  and grays, heavy atmosphere, slow dolly-in, no music swell — dread
  through stillness, not motion.
- [ ] **Vrakk's confrontation** (`goblin_boss_confrontation_script`) —
  A goblin warlord standing in a torch-lit underground warren hub,
  arms crossed, unbothered by the party's arrival. Warm torchlight,
  rough-hewn stone, crude banners. He doesn't attack immediately —
  resigned posture, not aggressive. Static wide shot, slow zoom in on
  his face as he speaks.

## Chapter 2 — The Descent

- [ ] **Glimmerdeep Grotto** (`vesh_grotto_conversation_scene`) — Vast
  underground cavern lit by slow-pulsing bioluminescent crystals in
  soft blue, a few glowing a wrong, sickly color. A lone figure
  silhouetted against the glow, not moving. Camera drifts slowly
  through the cavern, crystals pulsing like a heartbeat, before
  settling on the figure. Cold, blue-lit, quiet, awe more than fear.
- [ ] **The Hush Below arrival** (`hush_below_arrival_scene`) — A
  pitch-black cavern where footstep echoes visibly lag half a second
  behind the character's steps (visualize as a faint light-trail
  delay). Absolute silence. Slow first-person push forward into
  darkness. Unsettling, minimal, no creatures visible.

## Chapter 3 — What Was Buried

- [ ] **The First City reveal** (`first_city_arrival_scenes`) — A
  narrow tunnel opens suddenly onto an impossibly vast buried
  cityscape — towers, streets, all carved in an unfamiliar script that
  reads like tally-marks repeated endlessly. A massive central spire,
  faintly glowing warm at its core like something breathing. Camera
  pulls back from a tight tunnel shot to a huge wide reveal as the
  city opens up. Awe, scale, ancient dread.
- [ ] **The Waking Ember's intro** (`the_waking_ember_confrontation_script`)
  — Deep in an ember-lit chamber, something vast and fire-wreathed
  slowly uncoils from stillness, embers scattering as it moves for the
  first time in an unknown span of time. No dialogue, pure primal
  motion. Orange/black palette, slow building intensity.

## Chapter 4 — What Waits Above

- [ ] **Ascending to the Unmoored Isle** (`unmoored_isle_ascent_scene`)
  — A small glowing shard held in an open palm, pulling upward on its
  own. Cut to: a tunnel opening onto open sky, camera rising fast
  through clouds to reveal a small island of grey stone, entirely
  untethered, drifting slowly above an endless cloud layer. A single
  still silhouette stands motionless at its center. Bright open sky
  contrasted against the underground chapters before it — the first
  real daylight-adjacent moment in the whole story so far.
- [ ] **The Waiting Shape** (`the_waiting_shape_confrontation_script`)
  — A tall, indistinct necrotic shape standing motionless on the
  drifting isle, facing away from the camera toward the party before
  they've even arrived. It slowly turns. No sudden movement — patient,
  ancient, unhurried dread. Desaturated, cold light.

## Chapter 5 — What the Warren Was Hiding

- [ ] **The Paymaster's Shadow** (`the_paymasters_shadow_confrontation_script`)
  — A humanoid shape made entirely of borrowed, flickering light/shadow
  seated behind an old ledger in a torchless underground room, lit by
  nothing visibly present. It doesn't rise. Cold, bureaucratic,
  unsettling stillness — the opposite of a typical "boss reveal."

## Chapter 6 — What the Roots Kept

- [ ] **The Keeping Current** (`the_keeping_current_confrontation_script`)
  — Water in an underground cavern moving unnaturally, flowing toward
  the camera/party rather than downhill, slow and deliberate rather
  than fast. No visible humanoid form — the water itself is the
  entity. Calm, patient, ancient. Blue-black palette, minimal light.
